## 2026-10-09 - Explicit aria-labels on action buttons and selects in lists and cards

Action buttons and select controls inside repeated list rows or grid cards (such as saved questions or dashboard cards) need explicit accessible names containing the specific item title context so screen reader users hear distinct, fully contextual labels (e.g. `Ver la pregunta «Controles»` and `Ancho de la tarjeta «Metas del año»`).
